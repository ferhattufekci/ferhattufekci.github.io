The site uses browser local storage for the selected theme (ft-theme) and whether the Calendly badge was dismissed (calendlyPermanentlyClosed). You can clear these values through your browser's site-data controls. Choosing an email, phone, social profile, or scheduling link opens the relevant external application or service. Information you submit through email or Calendly is processed by those services and the recipient. Do not send sensitive personal information or confidential project data in an initial inquiry.
