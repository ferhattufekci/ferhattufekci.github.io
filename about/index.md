# About Ferhat Tüfekçi

## Engineering across the lifecycle

Ferhat Tüfekçi is a computer engineer based in Ankara, Turkey. His published professional background spans systems engineering, business analysis, software architecture, full-stack development, and software testing. He describes this combination as an M-shaped engineering profile: breadth across the delivery lifecycle together with the ability to focus deeply on the role a team needs.

The portfolio documents work in mission-critical banking and defense environments, including experience with HAVELSAN and MilSOFT, freelance software projects, and STEM project management for the Ministry of National Education. It covers requirements analysis, traceability, architecture, Java-based backend services, enterprise interfaces, and quality engineering practices.
