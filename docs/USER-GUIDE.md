# User guide

**🇬🇧 English | [🇹🇷 Türkçe](USER-GUIDE.tr.md)**

How to use [ferhattufekci.github.io](https://ferhattufekci.github.io). The site has five
sections, all on one page, plus About, Contact and Privacy pages linked from the footer.

## Moving around

| Goal | How |
|---|---|
| Open a section | Use the menu on the left (on a phone: the ☰ button at the top right). |
| Next or previous section | The arrow buttons at the bottom right. They wrap from Contact back to About Me. |
| Share a link to a section | Copy the address bar: each section has its own address, such as `/#resume` or `/#blog`. |
| Go back | The browser's back and forward buttons return to the previous section. |
| Close the phone menu | Tap outside it, choose a section, or press Escape. |
| Wrong or old address | A "page not found" view offers links back to About Me and Resume. |

## Sections

- **About Me:** introduction and the profile summary.
- **Resume:** the full CV. The bar at the top (All, Summary, Top Skills, …, Certifications)
  jumps to a part without hiding anything. **Show More (+N)** opens the N further entries of
  a list; **Download Full Resume (PDF)** opens the PDF in a new tab.
- **Blog:** Medium articles, refreshed automatically every 6 hours. Filter by language
  (All / TR / EN) and by category. **Share** on a card offers platform links, **Copy link**,
  and on supported phones the device's own share sheet.
- **Projects:** public GitHub repositories, refreshed weekly. Filter by category; pinned
  repositories come first. **Starred** lists repositories Ferhat recommends, 20 at a time.
- **Contact:** phone, e-mail, profile links and an interview scheduler.

The badge next to the Blog and Projects filters shows when that data was last refreshed.

## Display

- **Light or dark theme:** the sun/moon button. The choice is remembered in this browser;
  without a saved choice the site follows your device setting.
- **Reduced motion:** if your device asks for reduced motion, sections switch without sliding.
- **Zoom:** the site stays usable at 200% browser zoom.
- **Printing:** each section prints its full content.

## Keyboard

| Key | Effect |
|---|---|
| Tab / Shift+Tab | Move between links and controls; the focused item is outlined. |
| Tab (first press) | Shows **Skip to content**; Enter jumps past the menu. |
| Enter / Space | Activate the focused button or link. |
| Home / End / Page Up / Page Down | Scroll a section's content when the content area itself is focused. |
| Escape | Close the phone menu or an open Share menu. |

## Without JavaScript, and for programs

- With JavaScript off, the profile, CV, article and project links remain readable as plain
  content; filters, theme switching and sharing need JavaScript.
- A Markdown copy of each page is published for tools and AI assistants: `/index.md`,
  `/about/index.md`, `/contact/index.md`, `/privacy/index.md`. `/llms.txt` lists them, and
  `/agent-instructions.md` explains how to use them.

## Privacy

The site uses Google Analytics, Google AdSense and Calendly. In your browser it stores only two
values: the theme choice (`ft-theme`) and whether you closed the Calendly badge
(`calendlyPermanentlyClosed`). Details are on the [Privacy page](https://ferhattufekci.github.io/privacy/).

## If something does not work

Reload the page. If a section stays empty, its data source may be temporarily unavailable;
the rest of the site keeps working. Problems can be reported through the
[Contact page](https://ferhattufekci.github.io/contact/).
